# LEGOLAND California — reference notes

Park facts used to drive the build (researched 2026-06-07 from legoland.com, Wikipedia, Coasterpedia/
RCDB, and the LEGO Galaxy press release). Used only as a private viewing reference — no copyrighted
art is committed (see DECISIONS LD2). Treat the three GPS anchors as exact; in-between land placements
are approximate.

## Geography
Park sits on a hillside, **entrance ("The Beginning") high to the NE**, lands radiating around a
**central lake** ("Lago"/Lake I, Coast Cruise boats) that sits **low to the south**. Real USGS relief
across the bbox is ~46 m (34–80 m); the three coasters span ~9.6 m (Coastersaurus 47.3 m <
Galacticoaster 50.9 m < The Dragon 56.9 m). Anchors (lat, lon):
- The Dragon — Castle Hill — 33.12903, -117.30995 (NE, highest)
- Galacticoaster — LEGO Galaxy — 33.12768, -117.31398 (west)
- Coastersaurus — Dino Valley — 33.12631, -117.31232 (south-central, lakeside)

## Coasters (rail rides; `config/rides.toml`)
- **The Dragon** (Castle Hill) — family steel coaster. Sequence: slow **indoor dark-ride** through the
  castle (guards → royal dining hall → dark tunnel → wizard's quarters → near-miss with the
  fire-breathing dragon) → **lift hill** → emerges outdoors → **drops & swooping turns around the
  keep** → back to station. ~20 riders/train, 40" min. *Phase-1 model:* stone keep with 3–4 round
  crenellated towers; rail enters a torchlit interior past a large green dragon (redstone/fire accent),
  exits, climbs a lift, does exterior drops.
- **Coastersaurus** (Dino Valley) — junior steel coaster past giant LEGO dinosaurs.
- **LEGO Technic Coaster** (Imagination Zone) — Mack wild-mouse; tight switchbacks, sharp drops; fastest.
- **Galacticoaster** (LEGO Galaxy) — indoor family space coaster (2026).
- **Cargo Ace** (Land of Adventure; *possibly Fun Town*) — small kids' tracked airplane coaster.

## Static rides (themed structures; visual-only motion where cheap)
Castle Hill: The Royal Joust. Land of Adventure: Beetle Bounce (drop), Dune Raiders, Lost Kingdom.
Pirate Shores: Captain Cranky's Challenge (swing), Pirate Reef. Fun Town: Driving School, Kid Power
Towers, LEGOLAND Express. Imagination Zone: Bionicle Blaster (teacups). LEGO Galaxy: G-Force Test
Facility, DUPLO Launch & Land. LEGO Movie World: Unikitty's Disco Drop, Queen Watevra's Carousel,
Emmet's Flying Adventure. Dino Valley: Explorer River Quest. NINJAGO World: NINJAGO The Ride.
